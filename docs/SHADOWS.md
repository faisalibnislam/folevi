# Shadows

Folevi has one shadow hierarchy, defined in `packages/design-tokens/src/tokens.json` (`shadow`) and generated by
`pnpm tokens` into `--shadow-*` (web), `tokens.ts` and `FoleviTokens.Shadow` (Swift). Audit and rebuild:
2026-11-28, after the owner asked to "make all the shadows consistent" and "create a consistent shadow hierarchy".

## How it works

A surface's shadow has two parts that are always written separately:

1. An edge: the outline. It never lifts anything.
2. A level: the drop shadow under the surface. Levels have no ring and no inset, so they combine with any edge.

On the web that reads as `box-shadow: var(--shadow-edge), var(--shadow-floating)`, or
`shadow-[var(--glass-edge),var(--shadow-floating)]` in a Tailwind class.

### Edges

| Token | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `hairline` | A 1px ring. |
| `edge` | A solid surface's outline: a white sheen along the top and a 1px ring. Folevi for Mac and iPad draw it as a 0.5px ring. |
| `control`, `primary` | Button skins (bevel, ring and a hint of drop). |
| `--glass-edge`, `--control-ring`, `--liquid-edge`, `--mac-glass-edge` | CSS-only edges for glass, fields and the iPhone's and Mac's glass. They live in `globals.css`. |

### Levels

| Level | Use it for | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flat | Wells, list rows, the content panel's neighbours: an edge with no drop. | none | none |
| `raised` | A control lifted off its track: the chosen segment, the active tab, switch knobs, slider thumbs, secondary buttons, chips, the iPhone's round plus buttons. | 1px 1px 4%, 1px 3px 8% | 20%, 32% |
| `resting` | A card on the page: note and folder cards, Home's cards, settings cards, the note's page, sticky notes, glass panels (sidebar, content). | 1px 2px 4%, 4px 12px -6px 8% | 24%, 36% |
| `lifted` | A card under the pointer, a selected card, anything being dragged. | 2px 4px 5%, 8px 20px -8px 14% | 26%, 46% |
| `floating` | Menus, context menus, dropdowns, popovers, tooltips, toasts, the note's bottom toolbar, the spelling suggestions box, the Ask Foli AI button, canvas toolbars, the iPhone's glass bars. | 2px 6px -2px 8%, 10px 28px -8px 16% | 30%, 50% |
| `overlay` | Dialogs, sheets, the command palette, the presentation slide. | 4px 12px -4px 8%, 18px 44px -14px 20% | 32%, 60% |

All drops are black. Dark mode needs stronger values to show at all; the edge does most of the separating there.
`apps/web/test/shadow-scale.test.ts` checks that every level is deeper than the one below it (offset, blur and
strength) in both themes.

## Rules

- Every drop shadow is a level. A raw blurred or offset layer (`shadow-[0_4px_12px_...]`, `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px
  ...`, an inline `boxShadow`), Tailwind's own sizes (`shadow-lg`) and the retired names fail
  `shadow-scale.test.ts`.
- Rings, inset lines, focus rings and 1px lines without blur are edges and are fine.
- Exceptions, listed in the test: drawing inside the folder and note art (the folder front's lip, a note's paper
  peeking out of a folder, the bookmark ribbon), the website (`components/marketing`, which still has its own
  scale) and the share images (Satori can't read CSS variables).
- Native code uses the same tokens: `.foleviShadow(.floating, radius:)` (edge plus level) or `.nativeShadow(.raised)`
  (drop only; `NativeShadow.card` is `resting`). Don't write `.shadow(color:radius:)` with numbers.

## What the audit found

About 190 raw drop shadows in the web app and 25 in the native code, most of them one-offs, plus four separate
scales: the web tokens (`card`, `pop`, `sheet`, `lift`), `--glass-shadow`, the iPhone's `--liquid-shadow` and the
Mac's own lighter `--mac-shadow-*` and `--mac-pop-shadow`. The same role often had three or four values.

Too heavy:
- Menus and popovers (`.ui-pop`): a 24px offset, 48px blur drop at 28%, plus a second ring on top of the glass
  edge. Now `floating`, one ring.
- Dialogs used the same menu shadow, so a dialog and a menu looked the same height. Dialogs are now `overlay`.
- Dark mode cards and folders: drops at 60% to 90% black (`0 14px 26px -14px` at 0.9 under folder cards).
- Theme previews in the inspector and gallery (35% and 45%), onboarding tiles (35% to 45%), the presentation
  slide (`0 30px 80px` at 45%), sticky notes (32% to 38%).
- The primary button's 25% glow.

Too light or missing:
- Menus and popovers on Folevi for Mac and iPad used `0 9px 20px` at 13%, lighter than some cards on the web.
  They are now `floating` everywhere, with the Mac's 0.5px rings kept.
- The People suggestions list in the Vault was a card with Tailwind's `shadow-lg`; it is now a menu (`ui-pop`).
- The Today/Home row arrows, the selected-note check and several chips each had their own value between 3% and 25%.

Same role, different values (now one level each):
- The note's bottom toolbar had its own `--shadow-dock`; canvas, flowchart, mind map, wireframe, sticky and insert
  toolbars had `0 6px 20px -8px` at 18%; the spelling suggestions box `0 8px 24px` at 14%; the Ask Foli AI button
  and the chat composer two more. All `floating`.
- Selected segments, active tabs, sidebar rows and knobs: eight variants of `0 1px 3px` between 3% and 25%. All
  `raised`.
- Note cards had a slanted paper shadow (`-3px 10px 14px`), folder cards a straight one (`0 14px 26px -14px` at
  42%), the page sheet a third. All `resting`, `lifted` on hover.
- Drag ghosts (a note, a tab, a folder drop): three values. All `lifted`.

## Platforms

- Web: `apps/web/src/app/globals.css` and the components. Windows and Linux (`apps/desktop`) show the web UI, so they
  get the same shadows with no extra work.
- Folevi for Mac and iPad's web frame keeps its 0.5px rings (`--shadow-edge` is a 0.5px ring under
  `[data-mac-native]`), with drops from the levels.
- Native (`apps/native-kit`): `FoleviShadow` and `NativeShadow` read the tokens; the note and folder cards, liquid
  glass, segmented controls, tooltips, switches and drag states use them.

## Not done yet

- The website (`components/marketing/marketing.css`) keeps its own scale and about 40 one-off shadows.
- `apps/native-kit/Sources/FoleviDesign/Generated/WebStyles.swift` holds shadows read from the web by
  `pnpm styles:gen`. It still has the old values until someone runs `pnpm styles:gen` against a dev server
  (it isn't checked in CI).
- PR 190 (Home: capture field, Today card, DNA map, summary card) adds its own shadows on another branch. When it
  lands, they should use `resting` (cards, capture field) and `lifted` (hover); the guard test will flag them.
- SwiftUI's `.shadow` has no spread, so native drops are a little wider than the web's.
