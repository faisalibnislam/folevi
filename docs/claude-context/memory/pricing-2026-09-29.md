Decided with the user on 2026-09-29, to replace the Basic/Pro personal plans and the Team/Business workspace plans. **Revised 2026-10-11** after a margin audit (30% profit on price, counting typing, graph building and Free users at 20:1): Free has AI only during the 7-day trial (0 monthly credits) and 500 MB; Core and Pro 10 GB; Pro AI $13.99/mo or $159/yr with 50 GB. Applied to everyone at once; existing Pro AI subscribers move to the new price at renewal, which needs the Polar prices changed by the owner. Also chosen: a note's knowledge graph rebuilt at most every 6 hours; typing-cost fixes. Declined: Core $2.55, Pro $5.45, Polar Growth plan, a monthly graph cap or charging graph to credits. The lines below are the 2026-09-29 plan; the revised numbers above win.

**Plans.** The same four plans (Free, Core, Pro, Pro AI) exist for Personal and for Team. Team plans are billed per member.
- **Free:** $0. 1 GB in total, shared by the owner's Personal and every free workspace they own, including all their members. Unlimited members. 25 AI credits per person per month. Cannot buy extra credits.
- **Core:** $1.99/mo or $19/yr (per member on Team); the owner chose these knowing the worst-case markup is 8% monthly and 15% yearly. 20 GB per person, NO AI: no credits, no packs, AI refused server-side and hidden in Core scopes; "No AI" is its selling point. Old Personal Basic maps to Core.
- **Pro:** $4.99/mo or $49/yr (per member on Team). The owner chose $49/yr knowing its worst-case markup is 23%, not 30%. 20 GB per person; in paid workspaces storage is NOT pooled. 180 credits per person per month.
- **Pro AI:** $12.99/mo or $149/yr (per member on Team). 50 GB per person. 550 credits per month, marketed as "Unlimited, fair use".
- **Credit packs:** Pro and Pro AI, personal and team, can buy packs: 500 credits $7.99, 1,000 credits $14.99. Valid 12 months; used after the monthly credits.
- Guests are free. Personal and team plans never affect each other. In a free workspace, members use their personal Free credits.

**AI credits.** One credit = $0.01 of real Gemini cost at the January 2027 price (3.8 Flash: $1.50 input / $7.50 output per 1M tokens; the price doubles on 2027-01-01). Deducted by actual token use.

**Payments.** Polar.sh, merchant of record. Starter plan fees: 5% + 50¢, plus 1.5% for international cards and about 1.25% for payout and FX. The owner has no operating costs or staff.

**Why:** The user wants the lowest prices that still give a 30% margin over worst-case cost (full storage, 20% download a month, all credits used, plus hidden Convex costs of typing and Free users).

**How to apply:** Never add plan features or limits that break this markup. Price changes must be re-run against Polar fees and Gemini 2027 prices. Related: [[production-setup-status]], [[no-em-dashes]].
