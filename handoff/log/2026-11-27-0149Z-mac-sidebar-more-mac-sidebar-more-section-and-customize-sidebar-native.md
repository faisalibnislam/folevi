# Mac sidebar: More section and Customize sidebar, native

- Date: 2026-11-27 01:49 UTC
- Area: mac
- Branch: claude/mac-sidebar-more

The Mac sidebar (apps/native-kit, WebSidebar+Mac.swift) now has the web's More section and Customize sidebar (PR 163), written natively: the layout rules in Model/SidebarLayout.swift (a port of apps/web/src/lib/app/sidebarLayout.ts with the same test cases), the saved layout in Model/SidebarLayoutStore.swift (read and saved through the engine's Settings Convex allowlist, viewsSettings.ts sidebarLayout.get and sidebarLayout.set, so the Mac and the web, Windows and Linux share one layout; local mode keeps it in the library), and the panel in Views/SidebarCustomize.swift (a sheet with a bordered list: a checkbox per item, drag to reorder, Move up and Move down, Reset to default, Done). More starts closed, remembers open or closed on this Mac (folevi.sidebar.more-open), and opens by itself when the page you're on is under it. Right-click an item for Move to More or Move to sidebar (Home and Drafts: Always on the sidebar) and Customize sidebar; right-click the sidebar's empty space, or the More row, for Customize sidebar. VoiceOver: each item has a Move to More or Move to sidebar action, More reads Expanded or Collapsed, the panel's rows have Move up and Move down actions and moves are announced. The iPad's native-look sidebar uses the same code. DemoEngine answers sidebarLayout.get and sidebarLayout.set for tests. Tested headless: swift test (Tests/FoleviMacTests/SidebarLayoutTests.swift, 16 tests: the layout rules with the web's cases, More's open state, saving and reading through the demo engine, the item menus, and the sidebar and the panel drawn offscreen light and dark); the whole native-kit suite passes (one scroll timing test, Tasks' fling, missed its budget once under load and passes alone). The iPad simulator build compiles. SwiftUI's accessibility tree can't be read from an offscreen window, so the VoiceOver labels and actions are untested; nobody has tried it in the app, with VoiceOver, or on an iPad yet.
