# Home: redesigned dashboard (greeting, Foli AI summary, capture field, Today timeline, customizable sections)

- Date: 2026-11-28 03:49 UTC
- Area: web
- Branch: claude/dazzling-sanderson-1d2975

The owner's Home design (https://claude.ai/artifact/4kYxTQcFRtsvwXydbTXc84, project/Main.dc.html, version 16, 28 November 2026), which grew out of the Home concepts canvas (the widget grid, the calm start's greeting and capture field, the briefing's Up next) through versions 14 and 16.

- Top line: the date at the left, Customize at the right.
- Opening, two columns (one under 860 px): at the left the greeting ("Good morning, <first name>", serif display, the one size past the type scale, listed in test/type-scale.test.ts), the Foli AI summary as the lede, the capture field, and Today's reviews when there are cards; at the right the Today card.
- Foli AI summary (only while Foli AI is on): a small "Foli AI summary · from N notes edited since Monday · updated 8:20" label with Refresh, a serif paragraph, three points each linking to its note (or to My tasks), "Ask a follow-up →" (/ai). New action ai.homeSummary (convex/ai.ts, the local backend's too), its prompt and parser in packages/ai-prompts (homeSummaryRequest, parseHomeSummary, tested in requests.test.ts); internal.ai.recent takes an optional since and counts the notes edited. Written once a day per place the first time Home opens, kept for the tab's visit in sessionStorage under the Catch me up prefix, so sign-out clears it. The Catch me up button left the web Home (CatchUp.tsx is unchanged, still the iPhone's).
- Capture field: "What's on your mind?" with Saves to (Drafts or any folder of any space, remembered on this device) and Add as a task, Start from a template, Save (imports.importText; Cmd/Ctrl+Return saves too).
- Today card: today's events still to come and the tasks due today or overdue on one timeline (lib/app/homeLayout.ts upNext), the first one still to come tagged "Up next · in 25 min" (upNextTag; "Now" while it's on), due tasks in red, event dots in their calendar's colour.
- Sections, full width, each a serif title over a hairline with See all, no boxed tiles: Recent notes (five note cards; four, then three narrower), Bookmarks (the same cards, with the bookmark ribbon), My tasks (one-line rows in two columns, the due date at the right, tick to complete), Recent folders (five folder cards), Foli DNA (this week's most linked note and its links, from aiGraph.graph). Add a block offers Shared with me and Templates (one-line rows like My tasks; a template starts a note) and Pinned note (one note kept as its card, picked from the recent notes).
- Customize: a dashed outline round each section with move earlier / later and take-off-Home buttons, an Add a block tile, Reset to the default Home. Saved per person; ids from the earlier designs (scratch, summary, upnext, today) are dropped from saved layouts.
- Backend: table homePrefs (profileId, order, hidden, pinnedNote) and convex/homePrefs.ts (get, setLayout, setPinnedNote), deleted with the account (maintenance.ts); local mode has the same (lib/local/functions/homePrefs.ts). Tests: tests/convex/home-prefs.test.ts, apps/web/test/home-layout.test.ts (layout merge, moves, hiding, Up next order and tag).
