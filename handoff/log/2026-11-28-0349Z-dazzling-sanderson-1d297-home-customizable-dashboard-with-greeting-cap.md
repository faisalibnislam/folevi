# Home: customizable dashboard with greeting, capture field, Foli AI summary, Bookmarks, Today and My tasks

- Date: 2026-11-28 03:49 UTC
- Area: web
- Branch: claude/dazzling-sanderson-1d2975

The owner combined three concepts from the Home concepts canvas into one design (https://claude.ai/artifact/4kYxTQcFRtsvwXydbTXc84, Main.dc.html, 28 November 2026): the widget grid (concept 1), the calm start's greeting and capture field (concept 4), and the briefing's Up next and Recent notes (concept 5), plus Foli DNA and Recent folders.

- Top: Customize at the right; the date and "Good morning, <first name>" centred; a capture field ("What's on your mind?") with "Saves to" (a picker of Drafts and the folders of every space the person is in, Personal and workspaces, remembered on this device in localStorage) and Add as a task, Start from a template and Save. Save makes a note through imports.importText (first line the title, the rest the body, as the Mac's "New note with text"); Cmd/Ctrl+Return saves too. The concept's Voice and Screenshot tools have no web feature behind them yet, so they're left out. Today's reviews stays under the field. The Catch me up button left the web Home: the Foli AI summary block replaces it (CatchUp.tsx is unchanged and still the iPhone's).
- Blocks, in a three-column grid (one column under 760 px), as design version 14 has them (the owner took the Scratch pad off): Recent notes (five real note cards, the full row; four, then three on narrower windows); Foli AI summary (span 2) and Bookmarks; Up next (today's events still to come and open tasks due today or overdue, in time order: lib/app/homeLayout.ts upNext), Today (the day's calendar events, externalCalendars.events), My tasks (open tasks soonest due, tick to complete with the Tasks page's useToggleTask); Foli DNA (this week's most linked note in the middle with its linked notes around it in their folder colours, from aiGraph.graph; a week without new links shows the most linked notes of all); Recent folders (one row of the real folder cards). Shared with me, Pinned note and Templates are off until added.
- Foli AI summary: one or two serif sentences on the week and three points, each linking to its note (or to My tasks), with "From N notes edited since Monday · updated 8:20" and Ask a follow-up (/ai) and Refresh. New action ai.homeSummary (convex/ai.ts, the local backend's too) with the prompt and its parser in packages/ai-prompts (homeSummaryRequest, parseHomeSummary, tested in requests.test.ts); internal.ai.recent takes an optional since and returns how many notes were edited. Home leaves the block out while Foli AI is off (arrangeHome's without), and writes the summary once a day per place, the first time Home opens, kept for the tab's visit in sessionStorage under the Catch me up prefix, so sign-out clears it.
- Bookmarks: five bookmarked notes, each a serif name, its folder and when it was edited, and a thin bar in the folder's colour; See all. Shared with me (sharing.sharedWithMe) and Templates (your templates, then built-in ones; a click starts a note from it) are drawn the same way. Pinned note: one note kept on Home as its card, chosen from the recent notes, Change to pick another.
- Customize: dashed blocks with move earlier / later and take-off-Home buttons; an Add a block tile with the blocks not on Home; Reset to the default Home. Saved per person.
- Backend: table homePrefs (profileId, order, hidden, pinnedNote) and convex/homePrefs.ts (get, setLayout, setPinnedNote), deleted with the account (maintenance.ts); local mode has the same in apps/web/src/lib/local/functions/homePrefs.ts (library meta). Tests: tests/convex/home-prefs.test.ts, apps/web/test/home-layout.test.ts (layout merge, moves, hiding, the summary left out while Foli AI is off, Up next order).
