# Home: redesigned dashboard (greeting, Foli AI summary, capture field, customizable sections with Today beside Foli DNA)

- Date: 2026-11-28 03:49 UTC
- Area: web
- Branch: claude/dazzling-sanderson-1d2975

The owner's Home design (https://claude.ai/artifact/4kYxTQcFRtsvwXydbTXc84, project/Main.dc.html, version 19, 28 November 2026, which the owner asked to apply), which grew out of the Home concepts canvas (the widget grid, the calm start's greeting and capture field, the briefing's Up next) through versions 14 to 19.

- Top line: the date at the left, Customize at the right.
- Opening, the page's full width: the greeting ("Good morning, <first name>", serif display, the one size past the type scale, listed in test/type-scale.test.ts), the Foli AI summary, the capture field, and Today's reviews when there are cards.
- Foli AI summary (only while Foli AI is on): a small "Foli AI summary · from N notes edited since Monday · updated 8:20" label with Refresh, a serif paragraph, three points each linking to its note (or to My tasks), "Ask a follow-up →" (/ai). New action ai.homeSummary (convex/ai.ts, the local backend's too), its prompt and parser in packages/ai-prompts (homeSummaryRequest, parseHomeSummary, tested in requests.test.ts); internal.ai.recent takes an optional since and counts the notes edited. Written once a day per place the first time Home opens, kept for the tab's visit in sessionStorage under the Catch me up prefix, so sign-out clears it. The Catch me up button left the web Home (CatchUp.tsx is unchanged, still the iPhone's).
- Capture field: "What's on your mind?" with Saves to (Drafts or any folder of any space, remembered on this device) and Add as a task, Start from a template, Save (imports.importText; Cmd/Ctrl+Return saves too).
- Today card (a section of its own, in the left column above My tasks): today's events still to come and the tasks due today or overdue on one timeline (lib/app/homeLayout.ts upNext), the first one still to come tagged "Up next · in 25 min" (upNextTag; "Now" while it's on), due tasks in red, event dots in their calendar's colour.
- Sections, each a serif title over a hairline with See all; the only tiles are the Today card and the DNA map: Recent notes (five note cards; four, then three narrower), Bookmarks (the same cards, with the bookmark ribbon), then one row of two columns (1fr / 1.15fr, stacked under 860 px) with Today above My tasks (one-line rows, the due date at the right, tick to complete) at the left and Foli DNA (this week's most linked note and its links from aiGraph.graph, a 288 px map) at the right, then Recent folders (five folder cards). The row is lib/app/homeLayout.ts homeRows: paired blocks next to each other share it, so moving one away puts each on its own row (My tasks then goes back to two columns). Add a block offers Shared with me and Templates (one-line rows like My tasks; a template starts a note) and Pinned note (one note kept as its card).
- Customize: a dashed outline round each section with move earlier / later and take-off-Home buttons, an Add a block tile, Reset to the default Home. Saved per person; ids from the earlier designs (scratch, summary, upnext) are dropped from saved layouts.
- Backend: table homePrefs (profileId, order, hidden, pinnedNote) and convex/homePrefs.ts (get, setLayout, setPinnedNote), deleted with the account (maintenance.ts); local mode has the same (lib/local/functions/homePrefs.ts). Tests: tests/convex/home-prefs.test.ts, apps/web/test/home-layout.test.ts (layout merge, moves, hiding, the paired row, Up next order and tag).
